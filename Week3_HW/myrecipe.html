Cheesecake

With this foolproof recipe, a creamy, beautiful, crack-free homemade cheesecake is totally doable even for beginners!

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups (150g) graham cracker crumbs
  • 1/4 cup (28g) confectioners' sugar
  • 6 tablespoons (85g) butter, melted
  • 1/8 teaspoon table salt
  • Two 8-ounce packages (452g) cream cheese
  • 2/3 cup (133g) granulated sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. Select a pie pan whose inside top dimension is at least 9 inches. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
  2. Make the crust by stirring together all of the crust ingredients.
  3. Press the crumbs into the bottom and sides of the pie pan.
  4. Make the cheesecake filling by mixing the cream cheese and sugar until smooth, then mix in the eggs and vanilla.
  5. Pour the filling into the crust and spread it into an even layer.
  6. Bake for about 30 minutes.
  7. Remove the cheesecake from the oven and let it cool.
  8. Refrigerate until ready to serve.

Recipe Notes

Let the cheesecake cool before refrigerating. Serve with fresh fruit if desired.
