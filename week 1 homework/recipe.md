# Cheesecake

A **creamy**, *beautiful*, crack-free homemade cheesecake recipe.

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cups graham cracker crumbs
- 1/4 cup confectioners' sugar
- 6 tablespoons butter, melted
- 1/8 teaspoon salt
- 2 packages cream cheese
- 2/3 cup granulated sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. Mix the crust ingredients together.
3. Press the crust into the pie pan.
4. Mix the cream cheese and sugar until smooth.
5. Add the eggs and vanilla.
6. Pour the filling into the crust.
7. Bake the cheesecake.
8. Let it cool and refrigerate before serving.

## Notes

Serve with **fresh fruit** if desired.
